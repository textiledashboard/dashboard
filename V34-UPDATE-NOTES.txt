V34 — Adaptive Visibility for All Charts

- Production, Overview, Domestic Market and International Trade charts now use a shared adaptive scale engine.
- Normal value ranges remain on the standard linear scale.
- When the largest value is at least 25 times the smallest positive value, square-root visibility scaling activates automatically.
- Small positive bars receive a minimum visible size.
- Exact values remain available through chart tooltips.
- Adaptive charts show a clear scale note inside the graph.
- Grouped bars, standard bars, line charts, combo charts and stacked charts are covered.
- The upload wizard chart preview uses the same visibility rules.
